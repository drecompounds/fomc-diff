How concentrated the sample is:
   lower: 13 votes from 5 officials across 5 years; Stephen I. Miran is 6 of 13 (46%)
   raise: 13 votes from 7 officials across 3 years; Esther L. George is 6 of 13 (46%)
  the whole scored sample comes from 19 distinct meetings

Read this as description, not evidence:
  - 87 decision meetings, 31 rate changes, 27 with at least one dissent.
  - Longest run of identical decisions: 15 meetings. Regime switches: 29.
  - Meetings are not independent observations; the effective sample is nearer 29 than 87.
  - Dissents cluster by person and era -- five of 2016's are largely one official.
  - This is descriptive, not a test. No p-values, no significance claims.
  - A null result is the expected outcome and is published as readily as any other.
